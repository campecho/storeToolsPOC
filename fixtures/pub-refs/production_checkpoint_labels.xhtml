Production Checkpoint CoverBind Jog/tap pages while binding to ensure they are even. Bind one book. Are pages even and in the correct order? Was the correct size cover used? Once book is cooled, gently pull front and back pages to ensure they are fully glued. Use caution—CoverBind machine is extremely hot. Book spines will be hot when removed. ü !
